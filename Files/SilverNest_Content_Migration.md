# About SilverNest Home Care

SilverNest Home Care provides compassionate, professional, and personalized in-home care services designed to help seniors and individuals maintain independence, dignity, and comfort in the place they call home.

Our mission is to deliver exceptional care while providing peace of mind to families through trusted caregivers, personalized care plans, and consistent communication.

Whether a loved one requires a few hours of assistance each week or around-the-clock support, our dedicated team is committed to improving quality of life through compassionate care.

# Family Resources

## Signs Your Loved One May Need Home Care

- Frequent falls
- Missed medications
- Difficulty preparing meals
- Increased isolation
- Difficulty maintaining personal hygiene
- Missed appointments
- Memory concerns

## Benefits of Aging at Home

- Greater independence
- Familiar routines
- Increased comfort
- Improved emotional well-being
- Personalized support
- Family involvement

## Questions to Ask When Choosing a Home Care Provider

1. Are caregivers screened and trained?
2. Is care customized for each client?
3. How is communication handled with families?
4. What happens in emergencies?
5. Is care available on weekends and holidays?
6. Are care plans regularly reviewed?

# Frequently Asked Questions

## How quickly can care begin?
In many cases, care can begin within 24–48 hours following assessment.

## Are caregivers insured and screened?
Yes. All caregivers complete screening and background verification processes.

## Can care plans change?
Absolutely. Care plans are designed to evolve with client needs.

## Is care available overnight?
Yes. Overnight and 24-hour care options are available.

## Can family members receive updates?
Yes. Communication with families is a key part of our care process.
