## 2. ON-PAGE SEO IMPLEMENTATION

### Title Tags
✅ **Formula**: Primary Keyword + Brand + Benefit
✅ **Length**: 50-60 characters (will show in SERP)

**Examples**:
- Home: "Dementia Care & In-Home Services in Cobb County, GA"
- Services: "Home Care Services — Personal Care, Dementia Care & More"
- About: "About SilverNest Home Care — Founded by a Nurse Practitioner"
- Intake: "Free Care Consultation Request | SilverNest Home Care"

### Meta Descriptions
✅ **Formula**: Benefit + Call-to-Action + Location
✅ **Length**: 150-160 characters
✅ **Include**: Primary keyword, unique value prop, CTA

**Example**:
"Nurse Practitioner–supervised home care in Cobb County, Georgia. Specializing in behavioral Alzheimer's and dementia care. Licensed, bonded, HIPAA-committed. Free consultation."

### H1 Tags
✅ **Rule**: One H1 per page (usually same as page title)
✅ **Include**: Primary keyword naturally
✅ **Don't**: Stuff keywords or repeat multiple times

### Internal Linking
✅ **Link text**: Descriptive, includes keywords where natural
✅ **Targets**: Link to related high-value pages
✅ **Structure**: Home → Services → Specific Service → Intake

**Example linking strategy**:
- Home hero → Services page (learn more)
- Services page → Specific service cards → Intake
- About page → Home page (footer)
- All pages → Contact/Intake CTAs

## FAQs

**Q: How long before we rank for "dementia care Cobb County"?**
A: 3-6 months for initial rankings, 6-12 months for top 5. Local search is faster than national.

**Q: Do we need a blog?**
A: Helpful but not required. Better to have solid GMB profile and high-quality pages than a neglected blog.

**Q: What's more important: GMB or website SEO?**
A: For local services like home care, GMB is 60% of visibility. Website is 40%.

**Q: Should we do paid ads (Google Local Services Ads)?**
A: Consider after organic is established. Costs $15-100/lead but very targeted.

**Q: How often should we post on GMB?**
A: 1-2 times per week minimum. Mix of tips, team spotlights, service highlights.
