# SilverNest Home Care — Google My Business Setup Guide

## Quick Start (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Create/Claim Your GMB Profile
1. Go to https://business.google.com
2. Sign in with your Google account (create if needed)
3. Click "Create an account" or "Claim your business"
4. Enter business name: **SilverNest Home Care**
5. Search for existing listing (verify if one exists)
6. If not found, select "Create a new business"

### Step 2: Verify Your Business
Google will ask you to verify in one of these ways:
- **Postcard** (10-14 days) — Mailed to your business address
- **Phone** (instant) — If phone number is already associated with business
- **Email** (instant) — If business has website with GMB email

**Choose postcard** if starting fresh. Once received, enter verification code.

## Step 8: Google Posts (Weekly Strategy)

### What are Google Posts?
Posts appear on your GMB profile and Google Search results. They expire after 7 days, so post regularly.

### Post calendar (sample):
**Monday**: Tip/Educational Post
- Example: "5 signs of dementia in loved ones"

**Wednesday**: Service Spotlight
- Example: "Discover the benefits of companion care"

**Friday**: Team/Culture Post
- Example: "Meet our caregiver, Sarah"

### Post formula:
1. **Headline** (30 chars) - Catchy, benefits-focused
2. **Description** (300 chars) - Explain, include keyword
3. **Call-to-action** - "Learn More", "Call Now", "Book Consultation"
4. **Image** (1200x628px) - Eye-catching, branded

### Example posts:

**Post 1: Tip**
```
Headline: Managing Dementia Behavior
Description: Behavioral changes are common in Alzheimer's disease. Learn about de-escalation techniques from dementia care experts. SilverNest provides behavioral Alzheimer's care with NP supervision.
CTA: Learn More
Image: Calm caregiver with senior
```

**Post 2: Service**
```
Headline: Respite Care for Families
Description: Caring for a loved one with dementia is demanding. Take a break with professional respite care. Our Nurse Practitioner-supervised caregivers provide expert, compassionate relief.
CTA: Request Care
Image: Family smiling together
```

**Post 3: Culture**
```
Headline: Meet Our Team
Description: Our caregivers are nationally certified, trained in dementia care, and passionate about dignity. Meet the faces behind exceptional home care in Cobb County.
CTA: Learn About Us
Image: Team photo
```

## Step 9: Managing Reviews

### Encourage reviews:
- Email existing clients with GMB review link
- Include review link in thank-you emails
- Ask verbally during care (respectfully)
- Post review request on Facebook

### Respond to reviews:
✅ **ALL reviews deserve a response** (positive or negative)

**Template for positive review**:
```
"Thank you so much for taking the time to share your kind words! We're thrilled [family member name] is receiving excellent care. Our team is dedicated to providing compassionate, professional home care. Please don't hesitate to reach out if you need anything."
```

**Template for negative review**:
```
"Thank you for your feedback. We take your concerns seriously and would like to discuss this further. Please call us at (000) 000-0000 or email hello@silvernesthomecare.net so we can make it right."
```

**Timing**: Respond within 24-48 hours while fresh

## Optimization Tips

### For Higher Visibility:
1. **Keep profile 100% complete** — All fields filled = higher ranking
2. **Post regularly** — At least 1-2x per week
3. **Encourage reviews** — More reviews = higher ranking (up to a point)
4. **Respond quickly** — Quick responses show active business
5. **Update hours** — Especially if different from normal business hours
6. **Add service areas** — Don't leave gaps in service coverage
7. **Use keywords naturally** — In description, services, posts
8. **Respond to Q&A** — Shows you're engaged and helpful

### For More Calls/Leads:
1. **Make call button prominent** — Add phone to website
2. **Use local keywords** — "Dementia care Kennesaw" not just "dementia care"
3. **Add clear CTAs** — "Call now for free consultation"
4. **Highlight availability** — "Same-day consultations available"
5. **Show credentials** — Mention FNP-C, CDP® in description

## Common GMB Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Profile shows multiple times | Claim all duplicate listings, consolidate into one |
| Address not showing | Verify address exactly matches all citations |
| Posts disappearing | Posts only show for 7 days; post frequently |
| Low visibility | Ensure service areas are complete, increase review count |
| Wrong category | Update primary category to match service type |
| Messages not received | Enable messaging in settings, check notification settings |
