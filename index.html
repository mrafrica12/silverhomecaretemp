Personal Care

Why Families Choose Us

Our Standards

Caregivers You Can Trust

Every SilverNest caregiver passes our rigorous multi-step screening process — so you can focus on what matters most.

Background Checks

Comprehensive national and state criminal background screening, sex offender registry checks, and identity verification — completed before any caregiver enters a home.

  • National criminal database search
  • County court records review
  • Sex offender registry check
  • Identity & SSN verification

Professional Training

All caregivers complete ongoing education in elder care best practices, dementia support, fall prevention, and person-centered care before and throughout their service.

  • State-required home care training
  • Dementia & Alzheimer's care
  • Fall prevention & safety protocols
  • Medication reminders & documentation

CPR & First Aid Certified

Every caregiver holds current CPR and First Aid certification from an accredited provider, renewed on schedule. We keep seniors safe and families confident.

  • American Red Cross / AHA certified
  • Adult & senior CPR techniques
  • First aid & emergency response
  • Certifications renewed every 2 years

Ongoing Supervision

Care doesn't end at placement. Our supervisors conduct regular in-home check-ins, client satisfaction reviews, and performance evaluations to ensure consistent quality.

  • Regular supervisor home visits
  • Monthly client satisfaction check-ins
  • Performance reviews & coaching
  • 24/7 on-call support for families
