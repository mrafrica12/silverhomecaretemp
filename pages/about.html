Our Mission

Privacy Commitment

Our HIPAA Commitment

HIPAA-Committed Practice

SilverNest Home Care is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all client information in accordance with HIPAA standards. A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is available upon request. For questions about our privacy practices, contact us at hello@silvernesthomecare.net.

What This Means for You

  • Your loved one's health information is never shared without explicit consent
  • All staff receive HIPAA training and sign confidentiality agreements
  • Digital intake data is stored securely with access restricted to care coordinators
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties
  • Business Associate Agreements available for healthcare partners
  • Right to access, correct, or request deletion of your information
