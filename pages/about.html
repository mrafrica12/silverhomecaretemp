Certified Expertise

Our Credentials Matter

When your loved one has Alzheimer's or dementia, credentials matter. Any agency can say they specialize in dementia care. We can prove it with nationally recognized certifications held by our administrator and required of our caregiving staff.

CDP®

NCCDP Certified Dementia Practitioner®

The CDP® is one of the most widely recognized dementia care credentials in healthcare. It signifies comprehensive education in dementia care, a commitment to person-centered approaches, and ongoing professional development.

Held by our NP administrator. Required for caregivers assigned to dementia clients.

Renewed every 2 years · 10 CE hours required · nccdp.org

essentiALZ®

Alzheimer's Association Certification

The essentiALZ® certification is issued directly by the Alzheimer's Association — the nation's leading dementia research and care organization. It covers person-centered dementia care, assessment, daily living assistance, and managing dementia-related behavior.

Held by our administrator. Required for all dementia-assigned caregivers.

Issued by the Alzheimer's Association · alz.org/essentialz

FNP-C

Georgia Nurse Practitioner License

Our administrator holds an active, unrestricted Georgia Nurse Practitioner license — a clinical credential that most non-medical home care agencies simply cannot offer.

Licensed Clinical Supervisor. Verifiable at gcboard.georgia.gov

Licensed by the Georgia Board of Nursing

DCH

Georgia DCH Licensed

We are a Georgia Department of Community Health (DCH) licensed private home care provider serving Cobb County and the greater Atlanta metropolitan area.

Regulated and inspected by the state.

Licensed by Georgia DCH

These aren't just certificates on a wall. They represent a genuine commitment to understanding the disease, the person living with it, and the family caring for them. We don't just care for people with dementia. We study it, we train for it, and we earn the credentials to prove it — because your loved one deserves nothing less.

Privacy Commitment

Our HIPAA Commitment

HIPAA-Committed Practice

SilverNest Home Care is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all client information in accordance with HIPAA standards. A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is available upon request. For questions about our privacy practices, contact us at hello@silvernesthomecare.net.

What This Means for You

  • Your loved one's health information is never shared without explicit consent
  • All staff receive HIPAA training and sign confidentiality agreements
  • Digital intake data is stored securely with access restricted to care coordinators
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties
  • Business Associate Agreements available for healthcare partners
  • Right to access, correct, or request deletion of your information
