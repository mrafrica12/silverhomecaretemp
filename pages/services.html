Our Specialty

Behavioral Alzheimer's & Dementia Care

Behavioral Alzheimer's and dementia care is one of the most demanding — and most needed — services in home care. Families dealing with aggression, wandering, sundowning, or combativeness often feel abandoned by agencies that aren't equipped to help.

SilverNest is different. As a Nurse Practitioner–led agency, we understand the clinical complexity behind behavioral dementia. We don't just send a caregiver — we build an individualized behavioral care plan, match the right caregiver temperament to each client, and provide direct NP oversight every step of the way.

  • Individualized Behavioral Care Plan — Developed by our NP before the first visit, covering specific triggers, de-escalation strategies, and daily routine
  • Dementia-Trained Caregivers — Every caregiver completes our 40-hour training program with a dedicated Alzheimer's and dementia module before assignment
  • NP Clinical Oversight — Direct supervision and behavioral monitoring to adjust care as needs evolve
  • De-escalation Strategies — Proven techniques for managing sundowning, wandering, aggression, and other behavioral changes
  • Respite Care Available — Specialized dementia respite gives family caregivers real relief, knowing their loved one is in expert hands

Additional Services

Everything Your Loved One Needs

A full suite of support services designed to keep life comfortable, safe, and full of joy.

Transportation

Safe, reliable rides to appointments, errands, and social engagements — maintaining independence and connection with the community.

  • Medical appointment transportation
  • Grocery shopping and errands
  • Social outings and family visits
  • Pharmacy pick-ups

Meal Preparation

Nutritious, home-cooked meals tailored to dietary needs, medical requirements, and personal preferences — every single day.

  • Dietary-specific meal planning
  • Fresh meal preparation and cooking
  • Grocery list coordination
  • Special diet management (diabetic, heart-healthy, etc.)

Respite Care

Dedicated relief for family caregivers — scheduled or on-demand — so you can rest, recharge, and return refreshed.

  • Scheduled relief shifts (day or evening)
  • Overnight respite care
  • Weekend and holiday coverage
  • Emergency respite availability

Light Housekeeping

A clean, organized home environment supports safety, mood, and overall wellbeing. Our caregivers keep living spaces tidy and comfortable.

  • Dusting, vacuuming, and mopping
  • Laundry and linen changes
  • Dishes and kitchen tidying
  • Bathroom sanitation

Overnight Care

Peace of mind through the night — a caring professional present so your family can sleep knowing your loved one is safe and monitored.

  • Nighttime safety monitoring
  • Restroom assistance at night
  • Medication reminders
  • Emergency response readiness

Dementia & Memory Care

Specialized care for clients with Alzheimer's or dementia — structured routines, gentle redirection, and deep patience from trained caregivers.

  • Structured daily routines and engagement
  • Wandering prevention and safety protocols

  • Family education and communication

Live-In & 24-Hour Care

Round-the-clock care with a live-in caregiver or two-caregiver rotation for clients requiring continuous supervision. Ideal for post-surgical recovery, late-stage dementia, or high fall-risk clients.
