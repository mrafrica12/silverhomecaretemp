Our Specialty

Behavioral Alzheimer's & Dementia Care

Behavioral Alzheimer's and dementia care is one of the most demanding — and most needed — services in home care. Families dealing with aggression, wandering, sundowning, or combativeness often feel abandoned by agencies that aren't equipped to help.

SilverNest is different. As a Nurse Practitioner–led agency, we understand the clinical complexity behind behavioral dementia. We don't just send a caregiver — we build an individualized behavioral care plan, match the right caregiver temperament to each client, and provide direct NP oversight every step of the way.

  • Individualized Behavioral Care Plan — Developed by our NP before the first visit, covering specific triggers, de-escalation strategies, and daily routine
  • Dementia-Trained Caregivers — Every caregiver completes our 40-hour training program with a dedicated Alzheimer's and dementia module before assignment
  • NP Clinical Oversight — Direct supervision and behavioral monitoring to adjust care as needs evolve
  • De-escalation Strategies — Proven techniques for managing sundowning, wandering, aggression, and other behavioral changes
  • Respite Care Available — Specialized dementia respite gives family caregivers real relief, knowing their loved one is in expert hands
