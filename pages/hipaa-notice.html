HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective: January 1, 2024 · Last updated: January 1, 2024

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS UNDER HIPAA

This Notice describes how health information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

1. Who We Are

SilverNest Home Care is a Georgia-licensed private home care provider. We are committed to maintaining the privacy of your protected health information (PHI) in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and applicable state law.

This Notice applies to all health information we create, receive, or maintain in connection with the services we provide to you.

2. How We Use and Disclose Your Health Information

We may use and share your health information for the following purposes without requiring your separate written authorization:

For Treatment

We use your health information to coordinate and provide your care. This includes sharing relevant information with your physicians, specialists, therapists, or other providers involved in your care plan.

For Payment

We may use and disclose health information to obtain payment for the services we provide, including submitting claims to insurance companies or other payers.

For Health Care Operations

We may use your information for internal business activities such as quality review, staff training, care plan development, and agency administration.

As Required by Law

We will disclose health information when required by federal, state, or local law, or in response to a court order or valid legal process.

For Public Health and Safety

We may share information to prevent or control disease, report abuse or neglect as required by law, or respond to a serious and imminent threat to health or safety.

3. Uses and Disclosures Requiring Your Authorization

For uses and disclosures not described above, we will ask for your written authorization. You have the right to revoke an authorization at any time by submitting a written request to us, except where we have already acted in reliance on it.

We will never sell your health information or use it for marketing purposes without your explicit written authorization.

4. Your Rights Regarding Your Health Information

You have the following rights regarding your protected health information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of your health information that we maintain. We will provide it in the format you request when reasonably possible.
  • Right to Amend: If you believe information in your record is incorrect or incomplete, you may request an amendment.
  • Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: You may request a list of disclosures we have made of your health information for purposes other than treatment, payment, and operations.
  • Right to Request Restrictions: You may ask us to limit how we use or disclose your information, though we are not always required to agree.
  • Right to Confidential Communications: You may ask us to communicate with you in a specific way or at a specific location.
  • Right to a Paper Copy of This Notice: You may request a paper copy of this Notice at any time, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.

5. How We Safeguard Your Information

SilverNest Home Care implements administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your health information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, including:

  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorized care coordinators only
  • Secure, encrypted storage of electronic records in Google Workspace with restricted permissions
  • Staff training on HIPAA privacy and security requirements
  • Confidentiality agreements with all employees and contractors who handle PHI
  • Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with third-party service providers who access PHI

6. Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

We require Business Associate Agreements from vendors and service providers who handle protected health information on our behalf. A copy of our standard BAA is available upon request by contacting us at Info@silvernesthomecare.net.

7. Breach Notification

In the event of a breach of unsecured protected health information, we will notify affected individuals as required by HIPAA's Breach Notification Rule, including notification to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services as applicable.

8. Changes to This Notice

We reserve the right to change this Notice at any time. Any revised Notice will apply to health information we already hold, as well as information we receive in the future. The current version will always be available on our website and upon request.

9. Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with us or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
