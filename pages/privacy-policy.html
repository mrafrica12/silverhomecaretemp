Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2024 · Effective: January 1, 2024

HIPAA-Committed Practice

SilverNest Home Care is committed to protecting health information in accordance with HIPAA standards. For questions about your rights or our practices, contact us at hello@silvernesthomecare.net. A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is available upon request.

1. Who We Are

SilverNest Home Care ("SilverNest," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at silvernesthomecare.net and provides professional in-home care services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information you provide when you use our website or request our services.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit our intake or contact forms, we collect:

  • Name and contact information (phone, email, address)
  • Care recipient's name and your relationship to them
  • General care needs (selected from predefined categories — not free-text medical fields)
  • Preferred contact time and referral source

Important: Our web forms do not collect Social Security numbers, diagnoses, insurance IDs, medication details, or clinical health records. Sensitive health information is collected privately and securely through our care coordinators during consultation calls — never stored in web-accessible systems.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and pages visited. We use this data only to maintain site security and understand general traffic patterns.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics on informational pages (Home, Services, About) to understand how visitors use our site. Analytics are not active on intake or contact form pages. Google Analytics may set cookies — see Google's Privacy Policy for details. You may opt out via your browser settings or Google's opt-out tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your consultation request and contact you at your preferred time
  • To match you with the appropriate care coordinator and caregiver
  • To send you a confirmation email and care-related communications
  • To maintain records required for care coordination and legal compliance
  • To improve our website and service quality

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. How We Store Your Information

Information submitted through our web forms is stored in a secured Google Workspace (Google Sheets) environment, accessible only to authorized SilverNest care coordinators. Access is restricted by role-based permissions. We retain intake data for the duration of the care relationship plus applicable legal retention periods, after which it is securely deleted.

Detailed clinical information discussed during care consultations is maintained in separate, access-controlled care management systems — not in web-accessible databases.

5. HIPAA Commitment

SilverNest Home Care is committed to complying with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) as it applies to our operations. This includes:

  • Training all staff on HIPAA requirements and confidentiality obligations
  • Executing Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with applicable service partners
  • Implementing administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for Protected Health Information (PHI)
  • Limiting PHI access to personnel with a legitimate care need
  • Providing clients the right to access, amend, and receive an accounting of disclosures of their PHI

To request a BAA or exercise your HIPAA rights, contact us at hello@silvernesthomecare.net.

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies. Essential cookies support basic site functionality. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are used only on informational pages and can be declined through your browser settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Workspace / Google Sheets — for secure form data storage
  • Google Apps Script — for form processing automation
  • Google Analytics — for website traffic analysis (informational pages only)
  • Google Fonts — for typography (loaded from Google servers)

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent to processing where consent is the legal basis
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@silvernesthomecare.net with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to adults and their families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently received such information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website or services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
