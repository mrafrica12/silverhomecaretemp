Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SilverNest Home Care services, our process, and how we support families.

Getting Started with Care

How quickly can care begin?
▼

In many cases, care can begin within 24–48 hours following your assessment. We understand that you may need care urgently, so we prioritize quick turnaround. During your free consultation, we'll discuss your timeline and work to accommodate your needs.

What is included in the free consultation?
▼

Our free consultation includes a detailed discussion about your loved one's needs, health status, lifestyle, and goals. We'll ask about daily routines, medical conditions, and what matters most to your family. This helps us understand whether our services are the right fit and what kind of care plan would work best. There's no obligation—it's simply a conversation to help you make an informed decision.

How does the caregiver matching process work?
▼

After your assessment, our care coordinators carefully match your loved one with a caregiver who fits their personality, needs, and preferences. We consider factors like communication style, experience with specific conditions, language preferences, and availability. We believe the right match is crucial to a successful care relationship.

About Our Caregivers

Are caregivers insured and screened?
▼

Yes. All caregivers complete thorough screening and background verification processes. This includes:

  • Comprehensive background checks
  • Employment and reference verification
  • Professional skills assessments
  • Ongoing training and education

We're licensed, bonded, and insured for your peace of mind.

How are caregivers trained?
▼

Our caregivers receive ongoing education and training covering:

  • Senior care best practices and dignity standards
  • Dementia and Alzheimer's care techniques
  • Safety procedures and fall prevention
  • Emergency response protocols
  • Communication skills and empathy training

We're committed to keeping our team knowledgeable and compassionate.

Can I request a specific caregiver?
▼

Absolutely. If a particular caregiver is a great fit and availability permits, we'll do our best to honor your request for consistency. Many families find that having the same caregiver builds trust and deepens the care relationship. Just let your care coordinator know.

Care Plans & Flexibility

Can care plans change?
▼

Absolutely. Care plans are designed to evolve with client needs. As your loved one's health, preferences, or circumstances change, we adjust the plan accordingly. We review care plans regularly—typically every 30 days—and are always open to adjustments. Your family's feedback directly shapes how care is delivered.

How flexible is the scheduling?
▼

Very flexible. Whether you need care for a few hours per week, part-time support, full-time coverage, or 24-hour care, we adapt to your schedule. We understand that needs change over time, so we're happy to adjust hours, days, and shifts as required. Just let us know what works best for your family.

Availability & Support

Is care available overnight?
▼

Yes. Overnight and 24-hour care options are available. Whether your loved one needs nighttime supervision, assistance with sleep, medication reminders, or emergency support, we can arrange caregiving that fits your needs around the clock.

Is care available on weekends and holidays?
▼

Yes. We operate 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Care doesn't take time off, and neither do we. If your loved one needs support during evenings, weekends, or special occasions, we're here to provide it.

What is your emergency response protocol?
▼

We maintain 24/7 care coordination support for emergencies. If a situation arises, caregivers are trained to assess, respond, and escalate appropriately. We have direct communication with emergency services and your family, and we coordinate with your loved one's healthcare providers. Your safety and peace of mind are our top priorities.

Communication & Family Involvement

Can family members receive updates?
▼

Yes. Communication with families is a key part of our care process. We provide regular updates about your loved one's care, health, mood, and well-being. You'll hear from your care coordinator regularly, and caregivers are always available for questions. Keeping you informed and involved is essential to our approach.

How often will I hear from my care coordinator?
▼

You'll receive regular check-ins from your care coordinator. The frequency depends on your care situation, but typically you'll hear at least weekly, especially in the first month of care. We also conduct formal care plan reviews every 30 days and are always available if you have questions or concerns.
