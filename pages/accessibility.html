Accessibility Statement

Last updated: January 1, 2024

Our Commitment

SilverNest Home Care is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all people, including those with disabilities. We believe that every person deserves equal access to information about the care services available to their family.

We work continuously to improve the accessibility of our website and strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our target standard.

Measures We Take

To support accessibility, SilverNest Home Care takes the following measures:

  • Include accessibility as a consideration in all site updates and new content
  • Use semantic HTML elements throughout the site for proper structure
  • Provide meaningful alt text on all informational images
  • Ensure sufficient color contrast ratios between text and background
  • Make all interactive elements keyboard-navigable
  • Use ARIA labels and roles where native HTML semantics are insufficient
  • Design forms with clear labels, error messages, and logical tab order
  • Avoid flashing content that could trigger photosensitive conditions

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas of our site may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Known limitations include:

  • Some older PDF documents linked from the resources page may not be fully screen-reader accessible
  • Third-party embedded content (such as maps or calendar widgets) may have accessibility limitations outside our direct control

We are actively working to address these limitations and welcome feedback to help us prioritize improvements.

Supported Assistive Technologies

Our site is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack)
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Browser zoom up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
  • High-contrast and dark mode display settings
  • Voice control software (Dragon NaturallySpeaking)

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the SilverNest Home Care website. If you experience a barrier or have difficulty accessing any content, please contact us and we will make every effort to respond within 5 business days:

  • Email: Info@silvernesthomecare.net
  • Phone: (470) 460-8253 — Monday through Friday, 9am–5pm ET
  • Mail: SilverNest Home Care, 4875 Floyd Rd Ste 304 #1017, Mabelton, GA 30126

When contacting us about an accessibility issue, please describe the page or feature involved, the assistive technology you are using (if any), and the nature of the difficulty.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the relevant national or local authority responsible for web accessibility enforcement in your jurisdiction. In the United States, you may contact the U.S. Department of Justice ADA Information Line at 1-800-514-0301.
